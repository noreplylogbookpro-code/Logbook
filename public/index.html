Frequently Asked Questions

Does Logbook Plus require an internet connection to work?

No. Logbook Plus is an offline-first Android application. All expense and income records are stored directly on your phone in an on-device SQLite database.

How do backups and data recovery work if I switch phones?

You can generate encrypted ZIP backup archives at any time from Settings and restore them seamlessly onto any Android device.

What file formats can I export my financial reports in?

Logbook Plus exports accounting reports in Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), standard CSV, and PDF formats with custom date range filtering.
