Help Center & Knowledge Base

Everything you need to know about offline tracking, receipt management, Excel exports, and cryptographic backups.

What is Logbook Plus?

Logbook Plus is a digital logbook and ledger application for securely tracking income and expense entries with receipt photos, tags, and multi-currency details. It operates with a local-first philosophy on Android.

Is an internet connection required?

No. The core application is 100% offline-first. You can record transactions, organize categories, attach receipts, and view charts anywhere without cellular reception.

How do I export my data?

Open the app, navigate to Export in the navigation bar, choose your date range, and select your preferred format: Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), Microsoft Word (.docx), or PDF.

How can I restore my data on a new phone?

Install Logbook Plus on your new device, go to Settings → Backup & Restore, and select Import Backup. Enter your encryption password to restore all transactions and photos instantly.
