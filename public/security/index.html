Security Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

At Logbook Plus, security is built into our core architecture. We employ a local-first approach to personal expense tracking and backup logging, ensuring your records never leave your control without your explicit request.

AES-256 Client-Side Encryption

All database logs and entry photos created within the Logbook Plus app are locally encrypted at rest on your device. We use standard military-grade AES-256 encryption. Your master password/keys are hashed cryptographically and are never transmitted to our servers.

Secure Cloud Backups

If you subscribe to our Cloud Backup API, your backups are encrypted on your local device before transmission. The data remains fully encrypted during transit (TLS 1.3) and is stored in our secure, isolated cloud vault as an opaque, encrypted blob. Since we do not store your private keys, our team cannot read your logs or decrypt your records.

Reporting Vulnerabilities

We welcome security reports from independent analysts. If you find a potential vulnerability, please reach out to us at: support@logbookplus.co.in.
