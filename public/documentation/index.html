Product Documentation

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

Welcome to the official documentation for Logbook Plus. Learn how to configure your account, set up encrypted sync folders, restore backups, and manage your billing settings.

1. Getting Started

To begin tracking your expense logs and exporting files offline, download the mobile application directly on iOS or Android. No registration is required to run the Free Plan offline; all data points are stored securely on your local device.

2. Setting up Cloud Sync Vault

With a Premium Plan, you can enable auto cloud storage synchronization to back up your records automatically:

  1. Open the settings menu inside the mobile application.
  2. Tap Backups & Sync.
  3. Toggle Cloud Backup Sync to ON. Your device will generate a secure private key and initiate backup operations automatically.

3. Account Tiers & Quota Limits

Logbook Plus provides two membership plans to suit different requirements:

  • Free Plan: Support for local backups, up to 2 Excel or Word exports per month, and offline storage.
  • Cloud Premium Plan: Support for client-side encrypted cloud backups, unlimited exports (Excel, Word, PDF), tag customization, analytics dashboards, and auto-quota management.

4. Troubleshooting Sync Warnings

If you encounter sync warnings inside the mobile app, follow these steps:

  • Check Connection: Confirm your device has an active internet connection.
  • Verify Subscription: Access the *Billing & Subscriptions* section in your user profile to verify your premium subscription status is active.
  • Reset Sync Keys: If sync authentication fails, navigate to Settings > Backups > Regenerate Keys to renew credentials.

5. Data Retention & Security

All backups are client-side encrypted using military-grade AES-256 before transmission. If you cancel your subscription, your backup files will be retained in our secure vaults for 30 days to facilitate migration, after which they are permanently deleted from our servers.
